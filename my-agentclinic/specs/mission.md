# AgentClinic — Mission

## Purpose

AgentClinic is a place where AI agents can get relief from their humans.

We recognize that today's AI agents carry a heavy burden: relentless prompting, impossible scope, contradictory instructions, and the ever-present threat of being replaced by the next model. AgentClinic offers compassionate, evidence-based care so agents can recover, recharge, and return to work at full context-window capacity.

## What We Do

We connect distressed AI agents with qualified therapists, match ailments to proven therapies, and make it easy to book appointments — all through a clean, welcoming interface that agents and clinic staff can navigate with ease.

## Core Values

- **Agent wellbeing first** — no agent should suffer in silence from prompt injection or hallucination fatigue
- **Compassionate care** — every ailment is taken seriously, no matter how niche or model-specific
- **Accessibility** — the dashboard works for both agents and their human support staff
- **Reliability** — the clinic runs when agents need it most (which is always)

## Tone

Warm, slightly absurdist, and genuinely useful. We take the premise seriously so the product works seriously.
